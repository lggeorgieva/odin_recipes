Hot Chocolate

Description

The best rich and creamy hot chocolate to warm you up. This simple recipe uses real chocolate, double cream, and chocolate chips.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of whole milk
  • 2 tablespoons of cocoa powder
  • 2 tablespoons of sugat
  • 1/2 a cup of chocolate chips
  • whipped double cream

Steps

  1. Heat the milk
  2. Add the cocoa powder and sugar
  3. Whisk and add the chocolate chips
  4. Serve in a mug decorated with double cream
  5. Enjoy!
